# Privacy Policy – iNglishUkrainian **Last updated: October 2026** ## Overview iNglishUkrainian respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app uses device features and third-party services to provide language-learning, translation and speech-recognition features. ## Data Collection iNglishUkrainian does **not**: - Require account registration - Collect personal data for its own purposes - Track users - Use third-party analytics or advertising services - Access contacts, photos or location ## Audio and Microphone Access iNglishUkrainian may request access to the microphone and speech recognition for voice input and speaking practice. The app uses Apple's Speech framework to convert spoken words into text. Depending on device capabilities, language availability and recognition settings, speech recognition may take place on the device or use Apple's speech recognition servers. When Apple's servers are used, audio captured for speech recognition is sent to Apple for processing. The app's normal speech-recognition workflow does not save microphone audio as audio recordings. ## Translation and Third-Party Services The app uses Google's ML Kit Translation to translate between English and Ukrainian. ML Kit downloads the required language models to the device. Once the models are downloaded, translation is performed on the device and does not require sending the text being translated to Google's servers. An internet connection may be required to download the language models. The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising services. ## Children's Privacy iNglishUkrainian does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. ## Changes to This Policy If privacy practices change in future updates, this policy will be updated accordingly. ## Contact If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact: **Email:** 3k1m21@gmail.com
